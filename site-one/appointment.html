Bring with you

  • Your referral letter, if you have one
  • Insurance details, where the visit is covered

If it is urgent

Vomiting blood, black tarry stools, severe abdominal pain or new jaundice need same day assessment. Go to the hospital rather than waiting for a clinic appointment.

What the first visit looks like

Thirty minutes, and a plan you leave with

The consultation starts with your account of the symptom: when it began, what changes it, what you have already tried. Then examination, then a decision about which tests actually add something. Not every visit needs an endoscopy, and you will be told plainly when it does not.

You leave knowing what is suspected, what will confirm it, and when you will hear back.
