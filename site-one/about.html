Education

Trained in Istanbul, Nairobi and Johannesburg

Dr. Firoz graduated with his undergraduate medical degree from Istanbul University, then took his postgraduate degree in Internal Medicine at the University of Nairobi. He obtained his fellowship training in Gastroenterology at the University of the Witwatersrand in South Africa.

That order matters. Internal medicine teaches a doctor to read the whole patient; gastroenterology narrows the lens to one system without losing the wider view. Most digestive complaints turn out to be connected to something else: the thyroid, a medicine, the liver, the heart. The training to notice that comes first.

Skills and experience

Diagnosis you can see

Since 2005 he has treated gastrointestinal and liver disease and performed advanced diagnostic and therapeutic endoscopies and colonoscopies. Therapeutic means the procedure treats as well as looks: removing polyps, stopping bleeding, widening a narrowed oesophagus, relieving blocked bile ducts.

  • Consultant gastroenterology for adults, across the full digestive tract and the liver
  • Diagnostic and therapeutic gastroscopy and colonoscopy
  • Visiting rights at all the major hospitals in Mombasa
  • Gastroenterology consultations at the Aga Khan Hospital
  • Inpatient reviews and urgent endoscopy for admitted patients

Teaching

Honorary lecturer, ECSACOP

Dr. Firoz is an honorary lecturer on the postgraduate programme of the East, Central and Southern Africa College of Physicians. The region trains its own specialists, and teaching on that programme means the standard of care spreads well beyond one clinic in Mombasa.

Away from the clinic

The doctor who has time for the question after the question

Gastroenterology is a specialty of long relationships. Inflammatory bowel disease, chronic liver disease and surveillance after polyps are followed for years, not weeks, so the way a consultation feels matters as much as what is written in it.

Patients are told what was found, in words they can repeat to their family, and what happens next.
