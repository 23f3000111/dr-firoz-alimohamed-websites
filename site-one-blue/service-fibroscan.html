FibroScan

A simple, painless way to check your liver health, with advanced liver assessment at The Mombasa Hospital, Consultants Clinic, Suite 5.

Book a consultation

What is a FibroScan?

Liver disease can develop without obvious symptoms. Some people may feel well even when liver damage is beginning.

A FibroScan is a quick, noninvasive test that helps assess liver health. It uses specialised technology to measure liver stiffness and estimate the amount of fat in the liver. This information can help your doctor identify, assess and monitor liver disease.

Unlike a liver biopsy, a FibroScan does not involve a needle or removal of liver tissue. It is generally painless and usually takes only a few minutes.

Why is liver health important?

Your liver performs many important functions. It helps process nutrients, produces essential proteins, stores energy and removes harmful substances from the body.

Several conditions can affect the liver over time, including:

  • Fatty liver disease: Excess fat builds up in the liver. This is often linked to overweight or obesity, diabetes and metabolic syndrome.
  • MASH (Metabolic Dysfunction Associated Steatohepatitis): Fat builds up in the liver and causes inflammation and liver injury. Over time, this may lead to scarring.
  • Chronic viral hepatitis: Long term infection, especially with hepatitis B or hepatitis C.
  • Alcohol related liver disease: Liver damage caused by harmful alcohol use.
  • Liver fibrosis and cirrhosis: Scarring that can gradually interfere with normal liver function.

Many people with these conditions have no symptoms, especially in the early stages. Early assessment can help identify liver problems and guide appropriate care.

What does a FibroScan measure?

A FibroScan provides two main measurements.

Liver stiffness

Liver stiffness is measured in kilopascals (kPa). A higher measurement may suggest liver fibrosis, which means scar tissue has developed in the liver.

Fibrosis can worsen over time and may eventually lead to cirrhosis. Measuring liver stiffness helps your doctor estimate the likelihood of significant scarring and monitor your liver over time.

However, liver stiffness can also increase because of active liver inflammation, bile duct obstruction and other conditions. For this reason, FibroScan results must be considered together with your medical history, blood tests and any other investigations.

Liver fat

FibroScan can also measure the controlled attenuation parameter (CAP). This helps estimate the amount of fat in the liver.

This measurement may be especially helpful if you have:

  • Overweight or obesity
  • Type 2 diabetes
  • High cholesterol or triglyceride levels
  • Metabolic syndrome
  • Suspected fatty liver disease
  • Persistently abnormal liver blood tests

Together, the liver stiffness and CAP measurements give your doctor useful information about liver fat and the possibility of liver scarring.

Who may need a FibroScan?

Your doctor may recommend a FibroScan if you:

  • Have fatty liver disease.
  • Have diabetes, obesity or other metabolic risk factors.
  • Have persistently raised liver enzymes.
  • Have chronic hepatitis B or hepatitis C.
  • Have a history of significant alcohol consumption.
  • Need assessment or follow up for liver fibrosis or cirrhosis.
  • Have other risk factors for chronic liver disease.

FibroScan may also help monitor your liver while you receive treatment or make lifestyle changes.

What happens during a FibroScan?

A FibroScan is simple and usually comfortable.

  1. You lie on your back with your right arm raised above your head.
  2. A trained operator places a probe on the skin over the right side of your abdomen.
  3. The probe sends gentle vibrations through the liver and measures how quickly waves move through the liver tissue.
  4. The machine calculates your liver stiffness and, where available, your CAP measurement.
  5. A healthcare professional reviews the results alongside your medical history and other test results.

The examination usually takes about 5 to 15 minutes. The total appointment time may vary depending on your preparation and individual circumstances.

You will usually be asked not to eat for about three hours before the examination. Please follow the instructions given when you book your appointment.

Is FibroScan safe?

FibroScan is a noninvasive test. It does not use needles or ionising radiation and is generally well tolerated. When appropriate, it can be repeated to monitor changes in your liver.

In some cases, the measurements may be difficult to obtain or may not provide enough information. Your doctor may then recommend another test or additional assessment.

FibroScan does not replace all other liver investigations. You may still need blood tests, an ultrasound scan, specialised imaging or, in some cases, a liver biopsy.

What do the results mean?

These results help your doctor assess the likelihood of liver fibrosis and the amount of fatty change. The results must be interpreted in relation to the type of liver disease, the quality of the examination and your other clinical findings.

A high liver stiffness measurement does not automatically mean that you have cirrhosis. Similarly, a normal or reassuring result does not rule out every type of liver disease.

Your doctor will explain your results and advise whether you need further tests, treatment or follow up.

Can liver disease be prevented or improved?

Many people can improve their liver health by addressing the underlying cause and making appropriate lifestyle changes.

Depending on your diagnosis, your care plan may include:

  • Reaching and maintaining a healthy weight.
  • Eating a balanced diet and limiting excess sugar and highly processed foods.
  • Taking part in regular physical activity.
  • Managing blood sugar, cholesterol and blood pressure.
  • Avoiding harmful alcohol use.
  • Receiving appropriate treatment for viral hepatitis or other liver conditions.

In some people, liver fat and certain types of fibrosis may improve when the underlying cause is treated. Advanced liver disease requires specialist care and regular monitoring.

A FibroScan can help your doctor assess your liver and develop an appropriate management plan.

Take the next step towards better liver health

You do not need to wait for symptoms before taking action. If you have fatty liver disease, diabetes, obesity, abnormal liver blood tests or another risk factor for chronic liver disease, ask your doctor whether a FibroScan is suitable for you.

A quick conversation with our team can help you understand the test, prepare for your appointment and take an informed next step. Contact The Mombasa Hospital today to book your assessment or to ask any questions about the service.
