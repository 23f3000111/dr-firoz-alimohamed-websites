Endoscopy and colonoscopy

Procedures that allow doctors to examine the digestive system directly. They can help identify the cause of symptoms, diagnose conditions and, in many cases, provide treatment.

The small intestine

Capsule endoscopy

You swallow a small capsule containing a tiny camera and light, which takes thousands of pictures of the lining of your small intestine.

No sedationUnexplained anaemiaCrohn's disease

Acid reflux

Oesophageal pH and impedance studies

Specialised tests that measure how often stomach contents flow backwards into the food pipe, to help diagnose reflux and investigate persistent symptoms.

HeartburnRegurgitationChronic cough
Read more
The liver

FibroScan

A quick, noninvasive test that measures liver stiffness and estimates the amount of fat in the liver, without a needle.

Liver stiffnessLiver fatNo needle

Point of care ultrasound for the liver

A bedside imaging test that uses sound waves to create real time pictures of the liver and nearby organs.
